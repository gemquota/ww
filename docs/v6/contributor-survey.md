# Contributor Experience Survey — V6-C1#1

## Purpose
Measure contributor satisfaction and identify friction points in the contribution process.

## Survey Template

### Onboarding (0-3 months)
1. How long did it take to set up your development environment?
2. Was the contributing guide (CONTRIBUTING.md) helpful?
3. What was the hardest part of making your first contribution?

### Productivity (3-12 months)
4. How predictable is the PR review cycle?
5. Do you feel you have the right tools and access?
6. How confident are you in the test suite catching regressions?

### Retention (12+ months)
7. What would make you more likely to increase your contribution frequency?
8. Do you feel recognized for your contributions?
9. What's the biggest barrier to contributing more?

---

## Analysis Plan
- Survey every 6 months
- Target response rate: >30%
- Track NPS score over time
- Publish anonymized results
