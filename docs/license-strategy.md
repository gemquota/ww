# License Strategy

Addresses NEW-V6-C3#1 (Dr. Aisha Kabir).

## License Choice

WW Bridge is licensed under the **MIT License**.

### Rationale

1. **Permissive**: Allows commercial use, modification, distribution, and private use
2. **Compatible**: Compatible with GPL, Apache, and other common licenses
3. **Simple**: Short, well-understood, minimal compliance burden
4. **Adoption-friendly**: Low friction for corporate users and contributors

### What the License Means

| Right | Granted | Condition |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Use | ✅ Any purpose | Include copyright notice |
| Modify | ✅ Create derivatives | Include copyright notice |
| Distribute | ✅ Share with others | Include license copy |
| Sublicense | ✅ Use in proprietary projects | No requirement to share source |

### Contributions

All contributions are accepted under the MIT license terms.
Contributors must sign commits with a Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO)
to certify they have the right to contribute.

### Third-Party Dependencies

See `docs/license-audit.md` (generated by `scripts/license_audit.py`)
for a full audit of all dependency licenses.

### Commercial Use

The MIT license permits:
- Internal use in commercial products
- Integration with proprietary software
- Commercial hosting/SaaS offerings
- Selling support or consulting services

No commercial license or separate agreement is required.
